## THE RECOMMENDATION: BLACK SAILS (Starz)

**The Protagonist: Captain Flint.**
- **The Resonance:** He is the geometric midpoint between **Ragnar Lothbrok** and **Walter White**, but with **Rust Cohle's** education.
- **The Anomaly:** He is a highly educated British Naval Officer who became a Pirate King to wage war against the British Empire.
- **The Philosophy:** He gives monologues that rival *True Detective*.
    - *"They painted the world full of shadows... and then told them that the darkness is where we live."*
- **The Mars-Rahu:** He destroys everything he loves to achieve his goal. He is an engine of pure will.
- **The Connection:** 
    - You loved *Vikings* for Ragnar? Flint is Ragnar with a higher IQ and more artillery.
    - You loved *Utopia* for the "Greater Good" evil? Flint is doing exactly that.

**WARNING:** Season 1, Episode 1-4 has some "HBO Sexposition" (boring sex scenes to sell subscriptions). **PUSH THROUGH.** By Season 2, it is the best political drama ever written.

**Verdict:** It solves the "Mindhunter Problem" (No boring home life) and hits the "Utopia/Vikings" frequency (Building a new world through violence).
